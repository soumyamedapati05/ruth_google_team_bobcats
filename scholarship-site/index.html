Scholarships that fit who you are

Most scholarship searches start with a form. This one starts with a conversation. You tell us about yourself out loud, we ask a couple of follow-up questions, and we show you the scholarships you actually qualify for.

No accounts to set up first. No fifty-field profile. Just talk for a minute.

How it works

  2. 02 We listen
  3. 03 Your matches

01  Talk

Hold Space, or tap the mic, and answer one question: tell me about yourself. Say whatever feels true. Where you're from, what you study, what you do after class.

02  We listen

The light responds while you speak. When you finish, we ask one or two follow-ups to fill in what's missing, like your year or what you want to study.

03  Your matches

You get a short list, not a database. Every match comes with the reason it fits, pulled from what you said.
